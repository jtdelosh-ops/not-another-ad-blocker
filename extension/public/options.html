Unsupported syntax and safety omissions

Only the supported subset is applied. Advanced selectors, scriptlets, redirects and other unsupported syntax are reported. Related rules may be omitted conservatively when their exceptions cannot be represented. Exceptions are kept ahead of blocking rules when the browser's rule capacity is reached. Local rules take priority over subscription rules.

    Local filter list

    Up to 128 KiB and 2,000 nonempty lines. Import replaces only your previous local list. Subscriptions and site settings are preserved. Unsupported lines are reported below.

    Local import syntax and limits

    Network: domain-anchored host or literal path rules, exceptions prefixed by @@, and $third-party. Cosmetic: a tag/class/ID compound, optionally followed by one direct-child check such as div:has(> .ad-label). The child must include a class or ID. Selectors are limited to 512 bytes; nesting, attributes, and other pseudo-selectors/combinators are unsupported. Positive domain scopes are optional. Diagnostics identify syntax outside this subset.

    Cosmetic rules apply to the top document only. Child-based rules depend on the marker element staying present. Reload open pages after changing rules or site exceptions. Open the popup for network block counts and recent session activity. Cosmetic hiding is not counted. The basic element picker creates supported site-specific cosmetic rules.
